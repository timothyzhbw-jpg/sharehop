5 · Apps people chat in

actions="copy,@messaging" with brand-colors. Apps that can't take a pre-filled message (WeChat, Instagram, KakaoTalk…) copy the message and open the app; on a computer, WeChat shows the QR code instead. Buttons that can't work on this device are hidden.

6 · 中国常用 App

actions="@china"：微信、QQ（电脑上显示二维码，手机上复制后打开 App）、微博、小红书、QQ 空间、抖音、豆瓣。

7 · Plug in your own app

Register a target once with ShareWidget.registerShareTarget(), then use its id in actions. This one is a made-up campus forum.

8 · Every built-in app

All registered targets that work on this device. Resize to a phone or open this page on one to see the mobile set.
